Case study · Financial administration

From debt to a ₦6.2M portfolio in 18 months

Executive Assistant (Financial Administration) to a tech founder · May 2025 to present · Shared with the client's written consent, client not named

The challenge

In May 2025 the founder had no savings and no investments. Most of his salary went to repaying borrowed money, he was spending more than he earned, and he could not say where his money went each month.

What I did

I designed a zero-based monthly budget from scratch, set the savings targets and spending categories, and ran it every month: savings first, separate spending pots, every transaction logged and every balance reconciled to zero.

Key results, May 2025 to November 2026

  • Debts cleared, apart from one scheduled phone loan
  • ₦6.9M set aside over 16 consecutive months, never missing a target
  • Savings target raised from ₦300k to as much as ₦735k after his salary increase
  • Over ₦730k earned in interest and returns
  • ₦1.5M rent paid entirely from savings, with records that match his investment platform to the naira
